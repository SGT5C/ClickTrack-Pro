ClickTrack Pro
Features

Everything you need
to play in time.

A full-featured metronome built for musicians of all levels — from the practice room to the stage.

Precision Metronome

Free
  • 40–240 BPM with drag-to-adjust and tap tempo
  • 13 time signatures: 2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 5/4, 6/4, 7/4, 9/4, 11/4, 5/8, 6/8, 10/8, 12/8, and custom
  • Audible click with accent on beat 1
  • Haptic feedback — Off, Light, Medium, or Strong
  • Beat flash visual indicator
  • Background playback — the metronome keeps running when the screen turns off
  • AirPlay output routing to speakers and receivers

Song Library & Presets

Pro
  • Save unlimited song presets with custom tempo and time signature
  • Search Apple Music to add songs — tempo is detected automatically
  • Album artwork displayed on each preset
  • Organize presets into set lists or browse by artist
  • Drag to reorder within any list or set list
  • Apply presets with one tap, or via Siri voice command
  • Quick-launch any preset directly from a Home Screen widget

Subdivision Mixer

Pro
  • Layer eighth notes, triplets, and sixteenth notes over the main beat
  • Independent volume slider for each subdivision layer
  • Beat grid visualization shows the full rhythmic pattern
  • Auto-stop after a set number of measures, or loop indefinitely

Instrument Tuner

Pro
  • Chromatic tuner with real-time pitch detection via the device microphone
  • 40+ guitar tunings: Standard, Drop D, Open G, DADGAD, Open D, Open E, and more
  • 20+ bass tunings: Standard, Drop D, 5-string, 6-string, and more
  • Frequency lookup for any musical note from A0 to C8
  • All audio processing happens on-device — never recorded or transmitted
  • Microphone is only active while the tuner is open

Practice Tracker

Pro
  • Start and stop practice sessions with one tap
  • Tracks elapsed time, play count, and tempo range for every session
  • Set a BPM goal before or during practice with a visual progress bar
  • Add session notes by typing or via Siri
  • Browse recent session history with detailed stats
  • Daily practice time and consecutive-day practice streak
  • BPM progress chart shows tempo improvement over time

Band Sync

Free
  • Sync tempo with nearby musicians over local Wi-Fi or Bluetooth
  • Conductor mode: share your tempo with everyone in the session
  • Follower mode: lock to the conductor's metronome in real time
  • Nanosecond-precision beat scheduling for tight, sample-accurate sync
  • No internet connection required — entirely local network
  • See sync quality metrics (latency, jitter) in real time

Apple Watch

Free
  • Full metronome on your wrist — start, stop, and adjust tempo without touching your iPhone
  • Digital Crown for precision tempo adjustment
  • Stays in sync with your iPhone in real time
  • Always-On Display support for low-power tempo readout
  • Watch face complications: current tempo, time signature, playback state, and practice streak

Widgets

Free
  • Metronome Control — play, pause, and stop from your Home Screen
  • Preset Launcher — launch any of your saved presets with one tap
  • Practice Stats — today's practice time and current streak at a glance
  • Lock Screen widgets — tempo and playback status on your Lock Screen
  • Live Activity & Dynamic Island — real-time beat indicator while the metronome is running

Siri & Shortcuts

Free + Pro
  • 35 voice commands — control everything without touching your phone
  • 15 free: set tempo, time signature, haptics, accent, and Band Sync
  • 20 Pro: manage presets and set lists, control practice sessions, look up tunings
  • Works in the Apple Shortcuts app and with the iPhone Action button
  • All commands run entirely on-device

Accessibility

Free
  • Full VoiceOver support — every control has a descriptive label, hint, and value; state changes (playing, paused, stopped) are announced automatically
  • Adjustable swipe gestures let VoiceOver users increment and decrement tempo without leaving the control
  • Voice Control compatible — all buttons and interactive elements respond to spoken names, and 35 Siri shortcuts give hands-free control of the entire app
  • Dynamic Type — text and numeric displays scale with your preferred reading size across all screens
  • Dark Mode — follows the system appearance automatically, with a manual override in settings
  • Reduce Motion — all animations and beat flash effects are disabled when the Reduce Motion system setting is on
  • State is communicated with labels and icons alongside color — readable for users who are color blind or have low vision
  • Apple Watch: VoiceOver labels on all controls, Reduce Motion support, and haptic feedback as an alternative to audio clicks
