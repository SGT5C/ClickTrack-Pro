Short screen recordings showing how to use ClickTrack Pro's features on your iPhone.

Band Sync

Start a Band Sync session as the conductor and have nearby devices join as followers, locking their metronomes to the same beat over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth.

Conductor
Follower

Auto-Stop

Set the metronome to automatically stop after a fixed number of measures — useful for timed practice drills or recording short takes.

Subdivision Mixer

Layer eighth notes, triplets, or sixteenth notes under the main beat and balance each subdivision's volume independently with the mixer.

Presets & Set Lists

Create a preset, import a song from Apple Music to set its tempo automatically, and organize your songs into a set list for a show or rehearsal.

Practice Tracker

Track practice time automatically while the metronome runs, view your daily streak, and review past sessions.

Tuner

Open the instrument tuner to see live pitch detection, tune by ear with the needle display, and switch between chromatic and instrument-specific tunings.
