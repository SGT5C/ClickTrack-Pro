ClickTrack Pro for Android is currently in development. The questions below cover what we can answer right now — check back here for updates as launch gets closer.
Availability
When will ClickTrack Pro be available on Android?

We're actively porting ClickTrack Pro to Android. There's no firm release date yet — we'll announce it here and on the home page as soon as it's ready.

Which Android versions and devices will be supported?

We're targeting recent versions of Android on phones and tablets. Wear OS support is being considered as a follow-up — see Features for the current plan.

Will it be free, like the iOS version?

Yes — the plan is to mirror the iOS pricing model: a free tier with the core metronome and Band Sync, and an optional one-time Pro upgrade for presets, the tuner, practice tracking, and the subdivision mixer. See Features for details.

Cross-Platform
Will my iOS Pro purchase carry over to Android?

App Store and Google Play purchases are handled separately by Apple and Google, so a Pro purchase on one platform won't automatically unlock Pro on the other. We're looking into options here and will share details closer to launch.

Will my presets and practice history sync between iOS and Android?

Cross-platform sync isn't part of the initial Android release. It's something we'd like to explore afterward, depending on demand.

Can an Android device join a Band Sync session with an iPhone?

Cross-platform Band Sync between Android and iOS is a goal for the Android launch, but it depends on finding a connectivity approach that works reliably on both platforms. We'll confirm support as development progresses.
