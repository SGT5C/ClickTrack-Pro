Privacy Policy

Your data stays
on your device.

Draft — ClickTrack Pro for Android is in development. This policy will be finalized before launch.

ClickTrack Pro for Android is being built around the same privacy principle as the iOS app: a metronome with an instrument tuner and band sync features that does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Data We Do Not Collect

ClickTrack Pro will not collect your name, email address, location, device identifiers, or any other personal information. There are no user accounts, no analytics SDKs, and no advertising.

Data Stored on Your Device

Song presets, practice sessions, and settings will be stored locally on your device. This data is not accessible to us.

Cloud Sync

We're evaluating an optional cloud backup for presets and practice sessions, similar to the iCloud sync used by the iOS app. If included, it would store data only in your own account and would not be accessible to us. If you don't enable it, everything stays on your device.

Instrument Tuner

The tuner feature will use your device's microphone to detect pitch in real time. Audio will be processed entirely on-device and never recorded, stored, or transmitted anywhere. Microphone access will only be active while the tuner is open and listening.

Band Sync

The Band Sync feature will sync tempo with nearby devices over local Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. No internet connection will be used. Your device's name may be broadcast locally so that other nearby ClickTrack Pro users can identify and connect to your session. This name would only be visible to devices in your immediate vicinity while Band Sync is active and would never be sent to any server.

Purchases

In-app purchases will be processed entirely by Google through Google Play. ClickTrack Pro will not see or store your payment information.

Voice Commands

If Google Assistant support is included, voice commands will run through Google's Assistant infrastructure according to Google's Privacy Policy — ClickTrack Pro will not receive your voice recordings. Information returned by commands (tempo, practice stats, preset names, etc.) will be drawn from data already stored locally on your device.

Third-Party Services

ClickTrack Pro will not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services.

Children's Privacy

ClickTrack Pro does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

This is a draft policy describing our plans for the Android app. A finalized version will be posted here before the app is released, with an effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this draft privacy policy, contact us at sgt5c@icloud.com.
