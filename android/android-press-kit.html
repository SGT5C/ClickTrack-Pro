Short description (draft)

Copy
ClickTrack Pro is a professional-grade metronome, coming to Android. It's planned to cover the full practice toolkit in one app: a precision click (40–240 BPM, 13+ time signatures), a chromatic instrument tuner with 60+ guitar and bass tunings, a practice session tracker with streak counting, a subdivision mixer for eighth notes and triplets, and a local-network Band Sync mode for rehearsing with other musicians.

Key features (planned)

  • Precision metronome — 40–240 BPM, 13+ time signatures, tap tempo, haptic feedback
  • Chromatic instrument tuner — 60+ guitar and bass tunings, on-device pitch detection
  • Practice tracker — session timer, streak counter, BPM goal tracking
  • Subdivision mixer — eighth notes, triplets, and sixteenth notes with independent volume
  • Song library and presets — set lists, one-tap launch
  • Band Sync — local-network multi-device tempo sync over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth
  • Home Screen widgets
  • Google Assistant voice commands and App Shortcuts (planned)
  • No accounts, no analytics, no data collection

Pricing (planned)

ClickTrack Pro for Android is planned to be free to download, with the full metronome, Band Sync, and Home Screen widgets included.

A one-time Pro upgrade is planned to unlock the song library and presets, subdivision mixer, instrument tuner, and practice tracker — mirroring the iOS Pro tier. Pricing will be set per-region by Google Play.

Privacy

ClickTrack Pro for Android is being designed to collect no personal data, with no user accounts, no analytics SDKs, and no advertising — matching the iOS app's privacy approach. Draft privacy policy at android-privacy-policy.html.
