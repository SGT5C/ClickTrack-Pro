Getting Started
What's included in the free version?

The free version includes the full metronome (40–300 BPM), all time signatures including custom, tap tempo, haptic feedback, beat accent, Band Sync, the Apple Watch companion app, all Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, the Live Activity and Dynamic Island, AirPlay output, and 14 Siri voice commands.

A Pro upgrade adds the song library and presets, set lists, the subdivision mixer, auto-stop after N measures, the instrument tuner, practice tracking, and 20 additional Siri commands. See the full features list for a complete breakdown.

How do I upgrade to Pro?

Tap the Pro button or banner in the app to see the upgrade options. The purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store — ClickTrack Pro never sees your payment information.

Pro is eligible for Family Sharing, so everyone in your iCloud Family group can use Pro features with a single purchase.

How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new device?

Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used when you purchased Pro. Then open ClickTrack Pro and look for a "Restore Purchases" option in the app's settings. Your Pro status will be restored at no extra charge.

Metronome
Does the metronome keep playing when the screen turns off?

Yes. ClickTrack Pro uses iOS background audio, so the metronome keeps running even when your screen turns off or you switch to another app. Make sure your iPhone is not in Silent mode if you need to hear the click — or use haptic feedback instead.

How do I use tap tempo?

Tap the TAP button repeatedly in time with the music or performance. ClickTrack Pro calculates the average of your last few taps and sets the tempo automatically. Tap at least twice to register a tempo.

How do I change the click sound or turn it off?

In the main metronome view you can toggle the audible click on or off. You can also use a Siri voice command: "Turn off the click in ClickTrack". The haptic feedback setting is separate — you can have haptics with no sound, or sound with no haptics.

How do I use a custom time signature?

Tap the time signature display to open the time signature picker. Scroll to the bottom of the list to find the custom option, where you can enter any numerator and denominator you need.

Can I route the metronome audio to AirPlay or Bluetooth?

Yes. Tap the AirPlay button in the app to open the system audio route picker, then select your speaker, receiver, or Bluetooth device. The metronome audio will play through the selected output.

Presets & Set Lists
How do I create a preset?

Open the Presets tab and tap the + button. You can type a song name directly, or search Apple Music to find a song and import its metadata and tempo automatically. Requires ClickTrack Pro.

What is a Set List?

A Set List is a named group of presets — useful for organizing songs by band, setlist, genre, or show. You can create as many set lists as you need and move presets between them. In the Presets view, switch to Set List view using the grouping control at the top.

Are my presets backed up?

Yes. If you're signed in to iCloud, your presets and practice sessions are automatically synced across all your Apple devices using Apple's CloudKit. If you're not signed in to iCloud, everything is stored locally on your device only.

Apple Watch
How do I install ClickTrack on my Apple Watch?

The Watch app is included automatically when you install ClickTrack Pro on your iPhone. If it doesn't appear on your Watch, open the Watch app on your iPhone, go to My Watch → Available Apps, and tap Install next to ClickTrack Pro.

How do I add a ClickTrack complication to my watch face?

Long-press your watch face to enter edit mode, then tap Edit. Tap any complication slot, then scroll to find ClickTrack Pro. Available complications include current tempo, time signature, playback state, and practice streak.

Does the Watch app work independently from my iPhone?

The Watch app syncs with your iPhone in real time when both devices are nearby. Changes made on the Watch (like adjusting tempo) are reflected on the iPhone immediately, and vice versa. The Watch app requires the iPhone to be nearby and connected.

Widgets
How do I add a ClickTrack widget to my Home Screen?

Long-press an empty area of your Home Screen to enter jiggle mode, then tap the + button in the top-left corner. Search for "ClickTrack" and choose the widget style and size you want — Small, Medium, or Large depending on the widget type.

Available widgets:

  • Metronome Control — play, pause, and stop without opening the app
  • Preset Launcher — launch saved presets with one tap
  • Practice Stats — today's practice time and streak
How do I add a widget to my Lock Screen?

Long-press your Lock Screen to enter customization mode, tap Customize, then tap the widget area below the clock. Search for ClickTrack and add a circular or rectangular widget showing tempo, time signature, or playback status.

Why isn't my widget showing the current tempo?

Home Screen widgets update when you change settings in the app — usually within a second or two. If a widget looks stale, try opening the app briefly to trigger a refresh. Live Activity and Dynamic Island update in real time while the metronome is playing.

Band Sync
How does Band Sync work?

Band Sync uses Apple's Multipeer Connectivity framework to sync tempo between nearby devices over local Wi-Fi or Bluetooth — no internet connection required. One device acts as the Conductor and broadcasts its metronome. Other devices join as Followers and lock to the conductor's beat in real time.

How many devices can sync at once?

Up to 8 devices can be in a Band Sync session simultaneously. All devices need to have ClickTrack Pro installed and be within Bluetooth or Wi-Fi range of each other.

Can followers change the tempo?

No — in a Band Sync session, only the conductor controls the tempo. Followers receive the conductor's beat and sync to it. If you want to take over as conductor, the current conductor needs to end the session first, then you can start a new one.

Siri & Shortcuts
How do I set up Siri commands?

No setup is needed — ClickTrack Pro's Siri commands work immediately after installing the app. Just say "Hey Siri" followed by a command like "Set tempo to 120 in ClickTrack" or "Start practice in ClickTrack." See the full list of 35 commands.

Can I use ClickTrack commands in the Shortcuts app?

Yes. Open the Shortcuts app, tap + to create a new shortcut, then search for "ClickTrack" in the action library. All 35 commands are available as Shortcut actions. You can also assign a Shortcut to the iPhone Action button.
