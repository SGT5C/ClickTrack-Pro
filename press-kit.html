Long description

Copy
ClickTrack Pro is a full-featured metronome and musician's toolkit for iPad, iPhone, and Apple Watch. At its core is a rock-solid metronome: 40–300 BPM, 13+ time signatures (simple and compound, plus custom), tap tempo, beat accent, and four haptic-feedback levels. A sample-accurate audio engine keeps the click tight even in the background, and AirPlay lets you route sound to any speaker or receiver. The Pro upgrade adds a song library and preset system — search Apple Music to save songs with auto-detected tempo and album art, then organize them into set lists. A subdivision mixer layers eighth notes, triplets, and sixteenth notes over the main beat with independent volume control. The chromatic instrument tuner covers 60+ guitar and bass tunings with real-time pitch detection, all processed on-device. The practice tracker records session time, play count, tempo range, and consecutive-day streaks, with BPM goal tracking and session notes. The Apple Watch companion gives full metronome control on your wrist, with Digital Crown tempo adjustment and Always-On Display support. Four Watch face complications display current tempo, time signature, playback state, and practice streak. On iPhone, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, a Live Activity, and Dynamic Island keep your tempo and stats visible at all times. Band Sync uses Apple's Multipeer Connectivity to sync tempo with nearby devices over local Wi-Fi or Bluetooth — no internet required, with nanosecond-precision beat scheduling. ClickTrack Pro supports 35 Siri voice commands (15 free, 20 Pro) and integrates with the Shortcuts app and iPhone Action button. Available in English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Simplified Chinese. No accounts, no analytics, no personal data collected.

Pricing

ClickTrack Pro is free to download. The free tier includes the full metronome, Band Sync, the Apple Watch companion, all widgets and complications, AirPlay, and 15 Siri commands.

A one-time Pro upgrade unlocks the song library and presets, subdivision mixer, instrument tuner, practice tracker, and 20 additional Siri commands. Pro is eligible for Family Sharing. Pricing is set per-region by the App Store.

Privacy

ClickTrack Pro collects no personal data. There are no user accounts, no analytics SDKs, and no advertising. Presets and practice sessions are stored locally using Apple's SwiftData framework and synced privately via iCloud CloudKit when the user is signed in. The instrument tuner processes microphone audio entirely on-device and never records or transmits it. Full privacy policy at clicktrackpro.app/privacy-policy.
