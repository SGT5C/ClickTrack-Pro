Privacy Policy

ClickTrack · Last updated: May 27, 2026

Overview

ClickTrack is a metronome app. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Data We Do Not Collect

ClickTrack does not collect your name, email address, location, device identifiers, or any other personal information. There are no user accounts, no analytics SDKs, and no advertising.

Data Stored on Your Device

Song presets and settings are stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Apple Music

If you choose to search for a song, ClickTrack sends your search query to the iTunes Search API (Apple) to retrieve song metadata such as title, artist, and album artwork. No personal information is included in these requests. ClickTrack does not access, play, or store your Apple Music library or listening history.

Apple Watch

If you use the Apple Watch companion app, tempo and settings are synced directly between your iPhone and Apple Watch over your local network using Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. This data is not transmitted to any external server.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. ClickTrack does not see or store your payment information.

Third-Party Services

ClickTrack does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services.

Children's Privacy

ClickTrack does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in the future, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Contact
